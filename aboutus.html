About TypeUp

Welcome to TypeUp, your personal typing practice platform!

TypeUp is designed to help users improve their typing speed, accuracy, and confidence through regular practice. Whether you are a beginner or looking to improve your existing skills, TypeUp makes learning simple and easy.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help everyone develop faster and more accurate typing skills through simple and effective practice sessions.

What We Offer

  • 60-second typing practice sessions
  • Typing speed measurement in WPM
  • Typing accuracy tracking
  • Progress tracking and practice history

Why Choose TypeUp?

Consistent practice is the key to improvement. TypeUp helps you monitor your performance and encourages you to become a better typist one practice session at a time.
